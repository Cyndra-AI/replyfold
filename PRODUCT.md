# Product

Replyfold is an open-source support inbox for small teams. It connects the team's
own mailbox (Gmail, Microsoft 365 and Outlook, or any IMAP and SMTP account),
writes a draft reply for every new customer email from the team's playbook, and
sends only what a person approves.

## Users

Support agents and leads at small online businesses. They work through the inbox
for hours each day, mostly with the keyboard, on a laptop in daylight.

## Jobs

1. See which conversations need a reply, and which draft needs a person's judgment.
2. Read the thread and the AI draft side by side, fix it, and send it in seconds.
3. Teach the drafts: edit the playbook and accept rules learned from heavy edits.
4. Watch reply volume, first response time and how much the team edits drafts.

## Constraints

- Every send is a human action. Nothing goes out on its own.
- The app runs on the team's own server with the team's own credentials.
- Mock data only in screenshots and docs.
